DRAGONFALL ARENA - FINAL CLEAN ASSETS

Your previous project contained duplicate C# scripts and duplicate TextMesh Pro example files.

INSTALL:
1. CLOSE UNITY.
2. BACK UP YOUR CURRENT PROJECT.
3. In the project folder rename Assets -> Assets_OLD.
4. Copy the Assets folder from this package into the project folder.
5. Open Unity 6.6.
6. Wait for import/compile to finish.
7. Open Assets/game/Scenes/DragonfallArena.unity.
8. Press Play.

DO NOT copy Assets_OLD back into Assets and do not merge the old Assets folder.
